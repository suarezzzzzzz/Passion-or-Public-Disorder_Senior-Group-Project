# Philadelphia Sports and Traffic Stops Analysis
*Medhavi Jambhekar, Vijetha Ramdas, Charlotte Suarez, Jasmine Xu, Alejandro Zamudio*

## Project Overview

This project was completed as part of my Case Studies in Data Science course taken during my senior year at The University of Texas at Austin. The objective was to integrate the Stanford Open Policing Project (SOPP) traffic stop dataset with at least one additional public database to investigate a meaningful real-world question using relational data.

Our team investigated whether professional sporting events in Philadelphia were associated with changes in local police traffic stop activity. Specifically, we examined whether the timing and outcomes of Philadelphia Phillies, Eagles, Flyers, and 76ers games corresponded with differences in traffic stop frequency, location, and enforcement patterns.

## Research Question

**How do professional sporting events affect traffic stop activity in Philadelphia?**

To answer this question, we combined publicly available traffic stop data with historical schedules and results for Philadelphia's major professional sports teams. The project required extensive data cleaning, integration, and relational database design before statistical analysis could be performed.

## Project Components

This repository includes:

* **Data Cleaning**: One example of the preparation and transformation of a teams dataset to create a consistent format suitable for analysis.
* **Statistical Modeling**: An example of the analytical methods used to evaluate relationships between sporting events and traffic stop activity.
* **Final Report**: A summary of our methodology, analysis, findings, and conclusions.
